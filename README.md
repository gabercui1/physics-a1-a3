# IB Physics HL: A.1–A.3 study course

Open the course at https://gabercui1.github.io/physics-a1-a3/ . No account or password is needed.

The course follows the supplied teacher checklists for A.1 Kinematics, A.2 Forces and momentum, and A.3 Work, energy and power. It includes explanations, examples, embedded video segments, practice, and progress saved in the visitor's own browser.

Past papers opens a choice of Paper 1 or Paper 2. The reader shows one original-layout PDF question at a time, followed by a worked solution that opens only when selected. The IB-only bank contains 142 Paper 1 and 21 Paper 2 questions from the 37 distinct complete exam papers supplied for this project. Whole mixed-topic questions are excluded. TrIBe Physics packs remain separate.

Worked solutions were independently prepared for this study group; they are not official IB markschemes. Source references and original question numbers are retained alongside the new sequential bank numbers. A few ambiguous questions or approximate graph readings are explained in the relevant solutions.
